## Data & Storage

All user data is stored securely in your local database. All generated images are stored in the `/images` folder, and their metadata is appended to the `gallery.json` file.

## Image Generation

Image generation is powered by a locally hosted AI model (**Stable Diffusion v1.5** with **LCM LoRA**), with generation time averaging under 5 seconds per image.
